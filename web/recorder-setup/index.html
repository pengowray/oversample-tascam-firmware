Bat recorder setup by sound

Phone's clock  

Tascam X8 Oversample Bat Recorder

Playing the chime will not set the X8's clock until a future firmware patch.

To test the chime, record it on the X8 at 192 kHz. On a computer, run the decode_wav program on the WAV file.

  • Hold the phone's speaker still, 10 to 20 cm from the X8's microphones, with the volume up.
  • Use the phone's own speaker. Bluetooth adds delay and can remove the chime's 18 kHz tone.
  • If you hear no tune, check silent mode. iPhones use a side switch or Action button.
AudioMoth chime format by Open Acoustic Devices, used under the MIT licence.

Decimal degrees. Optional: fill in both or neither.

Optional. Exactly 16 hexadecimal digits.

If ticked, a 10 s tone plays before the chimes. Move the AudioMoth's switch to CUSTOM during the tone.

  • Hold the phone still, close to the AudioMoth's microphone, with the volume up. Let several chimes play. Field guides say the AudioMoth may not read the first chime.
  • Red LED on, green LED flashing occasionally: listening. Green flashing faster: receiving a chime. Green on for about 2 s: chime read.
  • Clock set: the AudioMoth listens for chimes for 30 s after the tone. Clock not set: it listens at CUSTOM with no time limit.
  • Configuration: the AudioMoth needs recording periods set through the AudioMoth Configuration App before it can listen.
The chime format is by Open Acoustic Devices, under the MIT licence.

This page has never been tested on a real PippyG.

Needed first: the PippyG is powered on, the start-up flashes have finished, and the red LED reacts to sound.

Trigger Level: Low (72) gives fewer recordings than High (24).

Device ID: 0 to 255. 0 keeps the PippyG's current ID.

Optional, in decimal degrees. If both fields are blank, each recording's metadata has latitude 0, longitude 0.

Reduces false triggers from constant noise. Firmware 8 uses this setting. Firmware 10 ignores it.

  • Success: the red LED flashes 8 to 10 times, then stays off. Firmware 10 also writes chirp.txt to the SD card.
  • Failure: no flashes, and the red LED keeps reacting to sound. Change the phone's volume and the distance, then play the chirp again.
  • Manual: about 4 cm from the microphone hole, 75% volume or more. Pipistrelle app notes: 10 to 15 cm, 25% to 33% volume; quieter is often better.
  • Power: once programmed, the PippyG stops listening until it is powered off and on. Its settings are lost when the power is off.
